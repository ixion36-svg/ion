{# Threat Intel: one page of the CISA KEV catalog, its pager, and its status bar. Replaces five innerHTML sites across kevRenderStatus, kevSearch, kevRow, kevRansomware and kevDetail. Most of this region is carried over unchanged, because it was the best-written part of this page. Three judgements in it are deliberate and are kept word for word: * The empty state names which catalog version found nothing. A bare "no results" invites the reader to conclude the CVE is safe, when what they have learned is only that one snapshot does not list it. * "ransomware: unknown" is worded as CISA not having established use, not as having ruled it out. * References are selectable text rather than links, because ION ships to air-gapped sites where an external anchor is a dead end that looks broken. The one defect: kevSearch's catch rendered into the ti-empty class, which is the no-results styling, so a failed lookup read as an empty catalog. That is the worst possible confusion for this panel in particular, since its whole purpose is to answer "is this being exploited right now". Since the condensed page the table pages, 50 rows at a time, says how many rows it was cut from, and filters by date added as well as by term and ransomware use. It used to render the newest 50 and stop, so the other 1,600 entries were reachable only by guessing a search term. The pager buttons send the filter form with them (hx-include), so a page turn keeps the filter it was paging through. The status bar swaps out of band.
